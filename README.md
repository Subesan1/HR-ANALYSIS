# HR Power BI Analysis 📊

A comprehensive HR analytics project leveraging **Power BI** to visualize and interpret workforce data.

This repository provides insights into employee demographics, diversity, departmental distribution, geographic spread, and hiring trends for a workforce of **22,214 employees**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This analysis explores:

- Workforce size and distribution (HQ vs Remote)
- Employee demographics (age, gender, race)
- Departmental breakdown and job roles
- Geographic concentration and hiring trends
- Strategic recommendations for workforce growth and diversity

---

## 📍 Geographic Spread & Hiring Trends

### Workforce Concentration
- Ohio accounts for approximately 18,000 employees
- Heavy geographic concentration creates location risk

### Hiring Trends
- Hiring patterns from 2000–2020 show peaks aligned with business growth and economic cycles

### Recommendation
Diversify hiring across secondary hubs and expand remote hiring initiatives.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Priorities

- Reduce geographic dependency on Ohio
- Expand hybrid and remote work adoption
- Enhance diversity and inclusion initiatives
- Build structured career pathways for entry-level talent
- Align hiring plans with workforce forecasting and market demand
